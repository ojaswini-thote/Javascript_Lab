01 / ABOUT THE HACKATHON

From problem statements to real solutions.

Smart India Hackathon is a nationwide initiative that encourages students to work on pressing challenges through technology, creativity, teamwork, and product innovation.

◎

Problem-driven innovation

Explore challenges that may come from government departments, public-sector organisations, industries, or open innovation ideas.

⌘

Collaborative problem-solving

Bring together technical knowledge, design thinking, research, and teamwork to develop practical prototypes and solutions.

03 / THE JOURNEY

How innovation takes shape.

A simplified overview of the journey from understanding a challenge to presenting a solution. Actual SIH processes and timelines depend on the current edition's guidelines.

01

Identify

Understand a problem statement and the people it affects.

02

Ideate

Research the problem and develop a feasible solution with your team.

03

Build

Develop a prototype, test assumptions, and improve your approach.

04

Present

Explain the solution, its feasibility, and its potential impact.
